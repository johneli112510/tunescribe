How it works

1

Bring your music

Upload an MP3, WAV or M4A, or record straight from your microphone — singing, piano, guitar, anything.

2

AI finds the notes

An open-source neural network (Spotify's Basic Pitch) hears chords and melodies, then we detect the key and tempo.

3

Edit, play and export

Fix notes in the piano roll, hear it back at any speed, and download PDF, MIDI or MusicXML.

Sheet music

Grand staff for piano, or treble/bass clef for single instruments. Key, time signature and tempo are detected and adjustable.

Piano roll

Drag notes to move them, drag the end to resize, double-click to add, Delete to remove. Hands are coloured by your split point.

Exports

MusicXML for MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale and Dorico. MIDI for any DAW. PDF via your browser's print dialog.

Questions

Which engine should I pick?

Use AI for piano, guitar and anything with chords. Use Melody for singing, whistling or a single wind or string instrument — it is fast and works without an internet connection.

Why are some notes wrong?

Busy mixes, drums, reverb and very fast passages make transcription harder. Try adjusting sensitivity, then clean up in the piano roll. Setting the right tempo and "beat 1 starts at" makes the sheet music much tidier.

Where are my transcriptions saved?

On the server, in Cloudflare R2 storage — the original audio and every edit are saved automatically, so My library shows the same list on your phone, tablet and computer. A backup copy is also kept in your browser in case you go offline.

Can I paste a YouTube link?

Not in this version — download the audio you have rights to and upload the file instead.
